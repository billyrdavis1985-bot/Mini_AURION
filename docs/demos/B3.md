## Measurement

From the receipts (`task_end` minus `task_start` for wall,
`model_call` durations summed for work):

| Leg | Status | Spend | Wall | Work | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serial | partial | $0.0676 | 27 s | 26 s | 2/2 |
| Parallel | partial | $0.0805 | 21 s | 33 s | 2/2 |

Speedup 1.29x (27 s / 21 s). Work exceeds wall on the parallel leg
(33 s of model calls in 21 s of wall-clock), which is the overlap
itself, not timing noise. The parallel leg cost more because two
specialists each orient (list + read) where the serial leg's second
investigation reuses warmer context — gain here is time, not spend.

## Why partial

Both recorded legs are partial for the same systematic reason, not
flakiness: the synthesis model files its reply as prose, without the
JSON `evidence` array the critic requires, so every synthesis fails
`NO_EVIDENCE` and the brief falls back to the verified notes. The
format instruction lives only in the specialists' system prompt; the
synthesis call never sees it. That gap predates B3 (it is the deeper
cause of H2's "fallback is the likely path") and is now a backlog
row; fixing it is a prompt change with its own gate, deliberately
not smuggled into this transcript. Partial-via-verified-fallback is
the honest demo shape, as in B2.
